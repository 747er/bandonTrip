Readable at arm’s length

  • Compass 90px, inside the same 208px card. The rise is wider, not taller. Speed is 30px, direction 12.5px, and the wedge and chevron are larger.
  • Days are 18px numbers with 11.5px weekdays. The selected day takes a light fill, a copper outline and a copper underline.
  • Sync: a day tap opens on its tee time. Hour, stop, pin and playback all drive one time, so the map pin, the stop badge on the hour and the compass always agree.
  • Play: tap the compass. A small play mark sits under the direction, and while playing a copper arc around the ring tracks the day from 6 AM to 9 PM. It steps an hour every 0.7s, and any tap pauses it.
